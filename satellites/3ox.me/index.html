A 3ox is a cube — one agent, station, or set, with an identity that outlives whatever model or runtime is behind it today. This is where a 3ox becomes reachable by name.

APEX :: 3ox.me · NAMESPACES :: 1 · PROCESSES :: 7

How it works

  1. Claim a namespace. 3ox.me/<you>/ is yours — the list of every 3ox you run.
  2. Register a process. Each one takes a name and answers at <proc>.3ox.me, with its own origin and its own certificate.
  3. The page mirrors the disk. A 3ox's identity lives in its own cube; the page is generated from it, so the two cannot drift apart.
